## How a change lands

Sonnet builds on a worktree branch → the orchestrator plants its own fault and runs the full gate script
locally → the merged tree is SHOT at 390×844 and 1280×800 (`scripts/eyes-shots.mjs`, 18 states) and looked at, Opus 5.5
reviews the screenshots against a checklist → `--no-ff` merge with the numbers in the message → push → Pages publishes
after the fast checks job; CI's three-engine, gallery and verify jobs are the arbiter for browser suites.
